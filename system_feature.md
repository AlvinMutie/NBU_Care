# NBU Care - System Features Documentation

## 1. Overview & Command Center
The NBU Care Command Center provides a real-time, high-level overview of the neonatal ward's status. It is designed to give clinical leads and nursing in-charges immediate visibility into critical metrics.
- **Live Ward Census:** Real-time tracking of bed occupancy and patient capacity.
- **Clinical Metrics:** Dynamic indicators for Nurse-Patient ratios, critical care patient counts, and medication latency.
- **Workload Heatmap:** A visual density chart showing the busiest times in the ward based on patient admissions and clinical events.
- **Efficiency Analytics:** Tracking average response times and clinical throughput.

## 2. Ward Registry (Patient Management)
A comprehensive digital registry for managing all neonatal admissions.
- **Patient Admission:** Streamlined workflow for admitting new neonates with essential clinical data (Gestational Age, Birth Weight, etc.).
- **Active Registry:** A searchable list of all currently admitted patients with quick-access status indicators.
- **Clinical Profiles:** Detailed individual profiles for each neonate, including history, vitals charts, and clinical notes.

## 3. Medication Safety Pipeline (Pediatric Calculators)
A mission-critical feature designed to eliminate dosing errors in neonatal care.
- **5-Step Verification Protocol:** A structured workflow for calculating and verifying drug dosages.
- **Automated Dosing:** Converts prescribed mg/kg doses into precise mL drawing volumes based on pharmacy stock concentrations.
- **WHO Safety Standards:** Built-in drug formulary with default doses and frequency intervals.
- **Integrated Audit Logs:** Automatically logs every calculation performed for accountability and safety audits.

## 4. Shift Handover System
Ensures zero clinical gaps during nursing and clinician shift changes.
- **Comprehensive Handover Reports:** Digital recording of patient status, including the last set of vitals.
- **Vital Sign Tracking:** Monitors Temperature, Sugar levels, Oxygen Saturation (SpO2), Heart Rate, and Respiratory Rate.
- **Clinical Planning:** Section for recording current commentary and future clinical plans for each patient.
- **Lead Verification:** Supports clinical lead oversight during transitions.

## 5. Shift Planner & Duty Rota
A central tool for managing the neonatal unit's workforce.
- **Duty Scheduling:** Plan and schedule clinician rotations in advance.
- **Shift Allocations:** Manage Morning, Afternoon, and Night shifts.
- **Role-Based Assignment:** Assign specific roles (Consultant, Manager, Ward Nurses) to each shift.

## 6. Clinical Academy (Learning Management)
An integrated educational suite for continuous medical education (CME).
- **Interactive Flashcards:** Digital quick-reference manuals for Routine, Clinical, and Critical neonatal protocols.
- **Case Simulations:** Interactive clinical scenarios that allow staff to practice decision-making and dose calculations in a safe environment.
- **Core Ward Modules:** Specialized guides for Resuscitation Pathways and the "Baby Warm Chain" (Thermal Care).

## 7. Admin Portal
A secure management suite for hospital administrators and ICT support.
- **User Vetting & Approvals:** Manage new registrations and verify staff credentials.
- **Role Management:** Dynamically update user roles and access permissions.
- **System Directory:** A complete list of all registered staff and their status.
- **Patient Record Management:** Oversight of the entire patient database.

## 8. Clinical Monitoring & Alerts
- **Vital Signs Monitoring:** Real-time tracking of patient vitals with color-coded safety warnings.
- **Maternal Profiles:** Integration of maternal history into the neonate's clinical record for better diagnostic context.
- **Audit Trail:** A global audit log capturing all system-wide activities for security and compliance.

## 9. Core System Features
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Permissions-based UI that adapts to the user's role (Nurse, Consultant, Admin, etc.).
- **Adaptive UI:** Supports both Dark and Light modes for different clinical environments (e.g., night shifts).
- **Mobile Responsive:** Fully optimized for use on both desktop workstations and mobile tablets.
- **Real-time Search:** Lightning-fast search across patient records and clinical manuals.
